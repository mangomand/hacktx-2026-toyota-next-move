# Product brief

Toyota Next Move helps a non-expert perform a guided exterior/interior inspection and decide what to do next. The demo path is: Start scan → guided coverage → synthetic scan replay → vehicle map → findings → one clear recommendation.

The app communicates coverage and uncertainty clearly. It can report what is visibly observed, what needs review, and what remains unseen. It never claims hidden mechanical diagnosis from exterior imagery. Synthetic customer, vehicle, loan and lease data make the recommendation repeatable for judging while live capture remains available.
